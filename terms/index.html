Health and safety

HRVWave is a wellness app for relaxation and breathing practice. It is not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition, and it is not a replacement for advice from a doctor.

  • If you have a pacemaker, a heart condition, a breathing condition such as asthma or COPD, epilepsy, or very low or high blood pressure, or if you are pregnant, talk to your doctor before you start.
  • Breathe gently. If you feel dizzy, light-headed, short of breath or unwell, breathe less deeply or stop and rest. Seek medical help if it doesn't pass.
  • Don't use the app while driving, in water or in any situation where losing focus could be dangerous.
  • The numbers the app shows (heart rate, HRV, coherence, your optimal pace) are estimates for practice. They depend on your device, how you wear it and how still you are, and they may be wrong. Don't use them to make medical decisions.
